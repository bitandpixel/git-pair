## Author reply

Added. In the drill's navigation mode `d` and `u` move half a page beside `ctrl-d`/`ctrl-u`, the way the
preview overlay takes them, and the bar now reads `d/u ctrl-d/u half  f/b ctrl-f/b page`.

One wrinkle worth your eye: out in the columns `u` is the unreviewed preset, and on the file list `d` opens
the patch of the row under the cursor. Inside a drill neither key means anything else, and each screen's bar
names only the keys that screen reads, so `u` is "unreviewed" while you are choosing an end and "half a page
back" once you are in the commit list. I read that as the convention the app already keeps — `q` leaves a
region and `z` changes its size, and both mean different things in the list and over the overlay — rather
than a new one. Say so if you would rather keep `u` free for the preset everywhere.
